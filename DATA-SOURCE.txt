Bangladesh dataset source:
https://github.com/TanjidulIslamToha/bangladesh-postal-codes-database
Repository reports MIT license and 1,347 records (8 divisions, 64 districts).
This is a community-maintained dataset, not an official Bangladesh Post export.
All records are marked not independently verified in this project.

Bangladesh administrative divisions and upazilas:
https://github.com/open-admin-data/bangladesh-administrative-divisions
Dataset includes 8 divisions, 64 districts and 495 upazilas.
License: CC-BY-4.0. Adapted into data/bangladesh-upazilas.json with attribution.
This is an administrative reference dataset, not an official Bangladesh Post postal-code export. Postal codes are indicative and must be confirmed with Bangladesh Post.
