WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Global Postal Codes

Global Postal Codes is an online reference directory created to make postal code information easier to discover. Whether you are filling out a delivery address, checking a location or simply looking for postal information, our goal is to help you find the details you need with less effort.

Our directory is being developed to bring country and location information together in one convenient place. You can browse countries, explore available administrative areas and search for postal codes where records are available.

OUR PURPOSE

OUR DATA & COVERAGE

Building a Useful Worldwide Directory

Postal systems and address formats differ from country to country. For that reason, the amount of information available in our directory may vary by location. Bangladesh currently has the most detailed local postal-code coverage on this website. For many other countries, the directory may currently provide country and state or division information only, while more detailed postal datasets are being added progressively.

Some countries or territories may not use a postal code system. Where applicable, this should be treated as country-specific address guidance rather than an assumed missing code.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Accuracy, Updates & Responsible Use

We aim to keep the directory clear and useful, but postal codes, boundaries and delivery arrangements can change. Information may be incomplete, illustrative or awaiting verification. A listing on this website should not automatically be considered an officially verified postal record.

Before using a postal code for an important shipment, official document or business transaction, please confirm it with the relevant national postal service or delivery provider. Global Postal Codes is an independent reference project and is not an official postal authority or affiliated with national postal operators.
